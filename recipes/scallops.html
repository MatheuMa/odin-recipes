Garlic-Lemon Scallops

It’s easier than you think to make perfect scallops at home. This simple scallop recipe is quick, easy, and full of buttery and garlicky flavor.

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup butter
  • 3 tablespoons minced garlic
  • 2 pounds large sea scallops
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ⅛ teaspoon pepper

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Stir in garlic and cook for a few seconds until fragrant.
  3. Arrange scallops in a single layer in the skillet; cook until golden brown on one side, about 2 minutes. Turn scallops over using tongs and continue cooking until firm and opaque, about 2 minutes more.
  4. Transfer scallops to a platter, reserving butter in the skillet.
  5. Whisk lemon juice, salt, and pepper into butter; pour sauce over scallops to serve.
  6. Enjoy.
